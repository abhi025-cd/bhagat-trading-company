About Bhagat Trading

Rooted in Nashik. Trusted Across India.

Founded in 2019 by Shubham Bhagat, Bhagat Trading Company is based in Pimpalgaon Baswant, Nashik—the primary center of India's onion production. We supply farm-fresh onions, tomatoes, and watermelons directly to 100+ wholesale dealers, mandis, and export partners nationwide, built on fair grading, transparent spot pricing, and dependable logistics.

Situated in Pimpalgaon (B), Nashik—the undisputed epicenter of India's onion trade—our trading desk ensures direct farmgate aggregation, stringent quality grading, and seamless multi-axle freight to wholesale destinations nationwide.
